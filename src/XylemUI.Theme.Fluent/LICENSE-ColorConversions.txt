The color conversions of XylemUI.Theme.Fluent (CieLab.cs) and the curve of its brand steps (BrandRamp.cs) were ported from
colors/csswg.ts and colors/geometry.ts of the Fluent theme designer (microsoft/fluentui, commit
1f59fc2121f828b57713915f6aa035b4e77c7d47, packages/react-components/theme-designer; MIT, see LICENSE-FluentUI.txt). csswg.ts says it
combines files of CSSWG's CSS Color 4 module and of Lea Verou's css.land; geometry.ts says that many of its functions are ported
from three.js (MIT, retrieved on 14 October 2021). The notices of these three works follow.
